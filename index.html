About Us

Creating future ICT professionals through innovation, creativity, and practical learning.

ICT & Multimedia Department

At Muhanga Technical Cental, our ICT & Multimedia Department equips students with modern digital skills. We focus heavily on **Software Development**, teaching students how to build applications and solve problems using code.

Students learn in a practical environment where they gain hands-on experience in computer applications, graphic design, and digital communication using the latest tools.

Software Development
Multimedia & Design
Hands-on Coding

Networking Department

Building and securing the digital infrastructure of tomorrow.

Infrastructure & Hardware

The Networking Department focuses on the physical and logical connections that make the internet work. Students learn about hardware maintenance, server configuration, and network security.

Through practical sessions, learners interact with routers, switches, and cabling systems, ensuring they can design and troubleshoot complex network systems.

Server Management
Network Security
Hardware Repair

Building Construction Department

Developing master builders through technical expertise and hands-on craftsmanship.

Modern Building Technology

Our Building Construction department provides students with the practical skills needed for the modern construction industry. We focus on structural integrity, architectural accuracy, and industrial safety standards.

Students engage in comprehensive training that includes masonry, plumbing installation, and blueprint reading, ensuring they are ready to lead construction projects from the ground up.

Masonry & Bricklaying
Plumbing & Sanitation
Technical Drawing

Automobile Technology Department

Training the next generation of automotive engineers and professional technicians.

Automotive Engineering & Repair

Our Automobile Technology program provides students with practical knowledge of modern vehicle systems. From engine diagnostics to mechanical repairs, we ensure our learners understand the heartbeat of automotive machinery.

In our workshop, students get hands-on experience working with real vehicles, learning about transmission systems, suspension, and electrical components under the guidance of experienced mechanics.

Engine Diagnostics
Maintenance & Repair
Transmission Systems
Learn More About This Course

🚗 Automobile Technology Course

This course provides foundational knowledge in Automobile Technology.

What You Will Learn:

  • Vehicle systems and components (Engine, Transmission, Braking, Suspension, etc.)
  • Automotive diagnostics using OBD scanners
  • Electrical and electronic systems in vehicles
  • Engine repair and maintenance
  • Safety systems (ABS, Airbags, ESP, etc.)
  • Basic welding, metalwork, and auto body repair
  • Introduction to Hybrid and Electric Vehicles

Duration:

6 months (Theory + Practical training)

Benefits:

  • Recognized Certificate
  • Hands-on workshop practice
  • Better job opportunities in garages, workshops, and automotive companies

Food & Beverage Department

Mastering the art of culinary excellence and professional hospitality service.

Culinary Arts & Hospitality

The Food & Beverage department at Muhanga Technical Center focuses on both the creative and management sides of the hospitality industry. Students learn international cuisine, pastry making, and the sophisticated art of mixology.

Our training emphasizes hygiene, customer service excellence, and professional kitchen operations, preparing our graduates for careers in top-tier hotels and restaurants worldwide.

Culinary Arts
Mixology & Bar
Table Service
Pastry & Bakery

Professional Accounting Department

Empowering students with financial literacy and professional management skills.

Financial Management & Excellence

The Professional Accounting department at Muhanga Technical Center (MTC) is dedicated to producing competent financial professionals. Our students gain deep insights into bookkeeping, financial reporting, and auditing.

In this modern classroom environment, learners are equipped with both theoretical knowledge and practical accounting software skills to meet the demands of the current job market and business world.

Computerized Accounting

Mastering industry-standard software like QuickBooks and Sage to streamline financial records and data management.

Taxation & Auditing

Understanding tax compliance, regulatory frameworks, and the systematic examination of financial statements.

Business Management

Developing leadership and organizational skills to manage resources effectively within a professional business environment.
